Below is a detailed analysis of each page.

Focuses on operational efficiency categorized by the nature of the customer's inquiry.

4. Weekly Report

The most granular view, used for tracking performance fluctuations week-over-week.

Time-Series Data: Breaks down metrics by month and specific week-ending dates.

Resolution Stability: Shows that the Resolved Rate remains remarkably static at 0.69–0.70 across almost all weeks, suggesting a capped efficiency or a specific process limitation.

NPS Volatility: Highlights how NPS can swing significantly; for example, in March 2021, the NPS dropped to -20.93% in the final week, signaling a specific service event or issue during that period.

A specialized view for identifying long-term patterns and regional distributions.

Regional Filtering: Includes slicers for AMER (Americas), APAC (Asia-Pacific), and EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa) to isolate geographic performance.

Comparative Trends: A large line chart compares Total Calls against Calls Handled (PM) over time.

Volatility Visualization: The sharp peaks and valleys in the line chart help workforce planners see where staffing levels may have failed to meet sudden spikes in demand.
